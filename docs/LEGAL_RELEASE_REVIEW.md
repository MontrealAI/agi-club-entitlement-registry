# Legal review for this deployment

**Status: preparation for qualified counsel and the operator, not a completed legal opinion or regulatory approval.** This project cannot eliminate legal exposure through a disclaimer. Passing CI is not legal clearance. Complete this review before even a limited mainnet canary; record it as `legalReview` in the private external-evidence manifest.

The public [bilingual notice](../frontend/legal.html) describes the actual entitlement/request flow. It does not describe an operational admission service as research-only, waive mandatory rights, deny the product's factual descriptions or declare the wider membership business exempt from financial regulation. The [MIT license](../LICENSE) continues to cover the software and associated documentation, including commercial reuse. Do not import incompatible “all rights reserved”, no-fork or non-commercial restrictions from another project.

## Complete the review in this order

1. **Establish the facts.** Identify the actual service provider and event organizer, their legal authority, business contact details, intended jurisdictions and audiences, membership acquisition history, consideration paid anywhere, fees, rights, transferability, existing promises and marketing. ENS wallet control does not itself prove a corporate mandate. Record the actual event arrangements; the supplied IA 101 example and 50-place draft are not evidence of venue capacity or an organizer's agreement.
2. **Have qualified counsel review the whole arrangement.** Québec/Canadian counsel should assess the intended local offering and arrange other jurisdiction advice where needed. Include related membership/NFT sales, secondary-market promotion, fundraising, redemption, profit expectations and third-party statements, not just this nonpayable registry. Document the applicability and outcome of securities/derivatives, consumer, language, privacy, tax, sanctions and any money-service obligations. Do not call a missing assessment “not applicable”. Any exemption or registration relied on needs an actual legal basis and its conditions satisfied.
3. **Publish the missing operational facts before a live service.** Confirm the provider identity, required business address/telephone, responsible privacy contact/title, eligibility, event details, claim window, capacity, fulfillment times, cancellation/refund arrangements and support route. Update the public notice/privacy page and the actual benefit description accordingly. The repository copyright notice and existing email address alone do not establish all of these facts. Do not invent them or expose members' contact information.
4. **Reconcile legal text with behavior.** Review the complete French and English versions, the member acknowledgement, wallet prompts, advertisements and Eventbrite terms together. Preserve applicable consumer remedies, legal warranties and existing commitments. Verify that language access is adequate; an English link or an unchecked box is not a substitute for the required French materials. Avoid claims of regulatory approval, anonymity, absolute security, guaranteed profit or lifetime benefits. Technical capacity to revoke a claim is not permission to disregard a member's legal rights.
5. **Complete privacy operations outside the static app.** Establish the actual responsible privacy role and publish its title/contact; inventory hosting logs, email, organizer records, backups and Eventbrite processing. Define purposes, necessary fields, access restrictions, retention/deletion periods, rights and complaint handling, incidents and applicable privacy impact assessments, including transfers outside Québec. Keep ticket fulfillment separate from marketing. Do not reuse receipts as a mailing list. Record third-party responsibilities and agreements; the app's lack of contact storage does not remove these obligations.
6. **Resolve findings and bind evidence to this release.** Complete the public corrections first, rerun qualification/fingerprinting, then have the reviewer approve the exact final source and operating scope. Retain the review securely outside the public repository. Changes to the offer, operator, target territories, custody/payments, terms or processing require renewed review; source approval does not automatically cover future registry benefits.

## Private report and release gate

Use an actual written report, not a checkbox alone. It should identify the qualified reviewer and review date, final source SHA-256, notice version, operator, jurisdictions, reviewed facts/materials, conclusions and their limits, any applicable registrations/exemptions, resolved findings, outstanding blockers and the authorized operating scope. A counsel-approved clearance summary can reference underlying privileged advice; do not publish that advice in CI, GitHub or static assets. Get counsel's direction on privilege and retention.

Store the report under `.local/` and calculate the SHA-256 of its exact bytes. Copy `releases/external-evidence.example.json` to `.local/external-evidence.json` without overwriting an existing evidence file. Enter the real reviewer, file path and hash under `legalReview`. Set `PASS` only after completion and resolution of release-blocking findings. `NOT_EXECUTED`, missing/empty files, an incorrect hash or evidence for old source blocks preparation. Neither this document nor its public sources is a substitute for the report.

The release gate binds the private report hash alongside security and staging evidence into the deployment approval. It cannot authenticate a professional qualification, verify the truth of a report or grant a legal exemption. The root holder must review the evidence and approve the exact plan. No review authorizes custody, fundraising, token sales or broader business activities outside its stated scope.

## Acknowledgement without contact persistence

The member's acknowledgement explains the conditions, public claim and gas before the interface requests a transaction or private signature. It starts unchecked. Withdrawal, clearing, inactivity, navigation and wallet changes invalidate it and any pending preparation; a submitted transaction cannot be cancelled by clearing the page. It is not sent to the wallet, attached to the receipt, stored in browser storage, logged or written on-chain. It supplies no persistent proof of contractual assent and can be bypassed by direct contract callers. If counsel determines a separate agreement or retained acceptance evidence is required, arrange it in an appropriately reviewed external process before launch. Do not add identity/consent tracking to this static app.
